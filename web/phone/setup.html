CARHUD on your phone

Do this once per phone. It lets the phone share its location with the HUD and plan routes.

1. Connect to the car

In the car, join the Wi-Fi network CarHUD (the password is on the desk screen: System app). At home, the phone and the Pi just need to be on the same Wi-Fi. Phones keep using mobile data for the internet while on the car's Wi-Fi.

2. Trust the CarHUD certificate

Phones only share location with secure pages. The Pi makes its own certificate, and your phone needs to trust it once.

Download the certificate

iPhone:

  1. Tap the button above, then Allow.
  2. Open Settings, then tap Profile Downloaded near the top, then Install.
  3. Go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and turn on CarHUD.

Android:

  1. Tap the button above.
  2. Open Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate and pick carhud-ca.crt. (Wording varies by phone; search Settings for "certificate".)

3. Open the app

Open CarHUD

Then add it to your home screen. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android: ⋮ → Add to Home screen. Allow location when it asks.

Bluetooth

Separately, pair the phone in its Bluetooth settings: on the desk screen, open Phone → Pair a phone, pick CarHUD on your phone and confirm the same code on both. That handles music, now playing, and (on iPhone) notifications and calls. On iPhone, also turn on Share System Notifications for CarHUD under Settings → Bluetooth → ⓘ.
